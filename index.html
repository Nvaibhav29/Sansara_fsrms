01 / WHY OYSTER

A different kind
of mushroom.

Oyster mushrooms have a delicate fan-shaped appearance and tender, savoury bite. They work beautifully in stir-fries, curries, soups and pan-seared dishes.

Compared with common white button mushrooms, oyster mushrooms can offer more dietary fibre and beta-glucans, while both remain nutritious everyday foods.
